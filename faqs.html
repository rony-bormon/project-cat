✦ Adoption Process

Start by completing our online Kitten Application on our website. Once your application is reviewed and approved, you can reserve an available kitten or join our waiting list for upcoming litters.
After approval, a non-refundable reservation fee of $350 is required to hold your kitten. The reservation fee is applied toward the final adoption price and your kitten is marked as reserved on our website.
Kittens are typically evaluated for structure and personality before we confirm placements. We will guide you through the process and help match you with the best kitten for your home.
Yes. When no kittens are immediately available, approved families may join our waiting list for future litters. We contact waiting list families in the order they joined when new kittens are available.
Yes. Every kitten is placed under a written contract that outlines expectations, health coverage, and care requirements. You can review our full contract terms on our Kitten Contract page.

✦ Pricing & Payments

Our pet kittens are $3,200. The price includes TICA registration, a 1-year written health guarantee, two rounds of core vaccinations, deworming, a starter pack, 1 month of free Trupanion insurance, and lifetime breeder support. Breeding rights are available to approved catteries for $5,500.
No. Reservation fees are non-refundable, as we take that kitten off the market and hold it specifically for you. The reservation fee is applied directly toward the total adoption price.
We accept commonly used methods such as cash, certified check, and select electronic payments. Details are provided in the adoption contract and on our Adoption Process page.
Final payment is typically due before or at the time of pickup, according to the terms in your written contract.

✦ Health & Veterinary Care

Yes. Our breeding cats are health tested for important genetic conditions common to the Maine Coon breed, including HCM, SMA, and PKD. Only sound, healthy cats are used in our program.
Kittens receive two rounds of core FVRCP vaccinations and are dewormed on a schedule recommended by our veterinarian. You’ll receive complete health records at pickup.
No. Pet kittens go home intact, and our contract requires that they be spayed or neutered by 6 months of age, with proof provided. Kittens sold with breeding rights ($5,500) are exempt.
At this time, we do not routinely microchip kittens. If microchipping is added in the future, it will be clearly stated in the adoption details.
Yes. Every kitten comes with a 1-year written health guarantee covering congenital and genetic conditions, along with complete health records from our licensed veterinarian.

✦ Transportation & Pickup

Our kittens go home between 12 and 14 weeks of age. By then they have had two rounds of vaccines, their veterinary checkups, and plenty of time to grow confident with people. We’ll confirm your kitten’s exact go-home date as it gets closer, and then plan pickup together.
Local pickup in Canton, Georgia is preferred. We can also meet within driving distance, or you may arrange a flight nanny to bring your kitten home safely inside the aircraft cabin. We’ll discuss the options once you’re approved.
No. We do not ship kittens as cargo under any circumstances. All transportation must be done in a safe, temperature-controlled, humane way.
We recommend bringing a secure hard carrier, a soft blanket or towel, and any remaining payment required at pickup. Detailed pickup instructions will be provided before your kitten goes home.
In some cases, we can hold a kitten briefly past the ready date. Any extended holding must be arranged in advance and may include a daily boarding fee.

✦ Maine Coon Care & Breed Characteristics

Maine Coons are a large, slow-maturing breed and continue to grow until approximately 3–4 years of age. Adult males typically weigh 16–25+ lbs, while females weigh 12–18 lbs. Size varies by line, gender, and individual genetics.
No cat is completely hypoallergenic. Some people with mild cat allergies tolerate Maine Coons better than other breeds, but individual biological reactions vary.
Yes. Maine Coons have long, dense double coats that shed seasonally. Regular brushing dramatically reduces shedding and prevents painful knots and mats.
We recommend brushing several times a week to keep the coat silky, healthy, and free of mats. Pay special attention to high-friction areas: the lion mane ruff, armpits, and back legs (pantaloons).
Maine Coons are famously gentle, social, and patient giants. With proper, gradual introductions, they do exceptionally well with respectful children and dog/cat companions.
Yes. We strictly require in our contract that our Maine Coons be kept as indoor-only pets or allowed outside only in a secure, predator-proof catio or on a harness and leash. This protects them from traffic, predators, contagious pathogens, and theft.
