Health & Genetic Testing

Distinctive Maine Coon Traits

Distinctive Maine Coon Traits

We celebrate the extraordinary genetic traits of European Maine Coons, including rare sapphire eyes and traditional snowshoe paws.

Rare Trait

Blue Eyes · Virginia

Blue eyes in a Maine Coon that isn't all white are genuinely rare. We breed thoughtfully for this trait. Ask us about upcoming blue-eyed litters.

Historic Trait

Polydactyl Paws · Kiki

Their charming "mitten paws" trace back to the breed's earliest working cats. Ask us about planned polydactyl pairings and current availability.
