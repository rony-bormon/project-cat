A Cherished Bond

The Heart of Our Cattery

At Majestic Maine Coon Cats, our retired adults are the foundation of our cattery’s legacy. These beautiful Maine Coons have already contributed their strength, health, and loving temperaments to the breed. Now, they enjoy a well-deserved retirement where they continue to live as cherished companions.

Each retired king and queen has been health-tested and carefully paired to ensure their kittens carried the best traits of the Maine Coon: intelligence, affectionate personalities, and stunning features. Beyond their breeding years, our retired cats remain gentle, loyal, and affectionate members of our family.

When you meet our retired adults, you are meeting the history of Majestic Maine Coon Cats — cats that shaped future generations and still embody the beauty, health, and heart of the Maine Coon breed.

“Forever cherished, our retired adults carry with them the grace, wisdom, and love that shaped Majestic Maine Coon Cats.”
Why Choose an Adult

Thank you for considering a retired adult

Thank you for taking the time to meet our retired adults.
