TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF SALE

1. Reservation Fee ($350, Non-Refundable)

A non-refundable $350 reservation fee is required to reserve a kitten and remove that kitten from availability. This fee is applied toward the total purchase price. If the Buyer later cancels the purchase for any reason, the reservation fee is forfeited. If the Seller cancels, any payments made by the Buyer are fully refunded.

2. Approval & Pick Order

Once your application is approved and the $350 reservation fee is received, your pick order is secured for the selected kitten or upcoming litter. This fee is non-refundable if the Buyer cancels.

3. Pet-Only Unless Approved for Breeding

All kittens are sold as pets only unless breeding rights are expressly approved in writing, an additional breeding fee is paid ($5,500 total), and a separate breeding contract is signed.

4. Mandatory Veterinary Examination (Within 48 Hours)

Buyer must take the kitten to a licensed veterinarian within 48 hours of pickup. Failure to do so voids the health guarantee. Minor transition issues (e.g., mild stress-induced loose stool or adjustment changes) are not grounds for refund or replacement.

5. Spay/Neuter Requirement (By 6 Months)

Buyer agrees the kitten will be spayed or neutered no later than 6 months of age. Official veterinary proof of sterilization must be sent to the Seller. Failure to comply constitutes a breach of contract.

6. Indoor-Only Requirement

The kitten will live strictly indoors. Outdoor access is only allowed in a secure, fully enclosed catio or with a harness and leash under direct supervision. Unsupervised outdoor roaming voids the health guarantee and contract.

7. Pickup Window & Boarding Fees

Pickup is in Canton, GA. Buyer must pick up within 7 days of the agreed release date. After 7 days, a $20/day boarding fee applies. After 14 days with no pickup and no communication, Seller may cancel and relist the kitten; all Buyer payments are forfeited.

8. Payments & Accepted Methods

Buyer must pay the remaining balance before or at the time of pickup. Seller does not accept personal checks or credit cards on pickup day. Approved payment methods include cash or verified electronic transfers.

9. No Refunds if Buyer Changes Mind

All payments made toward the kitten — including the reservation fee and any additional milestone payments — are non-refundable if the Buyer changes their mind for any reason.

10. Genetic Health Guarantee (1 Year)

Seller provides a 1-year genetic health guarantee for severe hereditary defects such as Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy (HCM) or severe hip dysplasia. Conditions caused by injury, neglect, stress, toxic exposure, or buyer actions are excluded.

11. Unfit-for-Sale Clause (72 Hours)

If within 72 hours a licensed veterinarian deems the kitten unfit for sale due to a severe congenital condition present at pickup, Buyer must provide written documentation. Seller may request a second veterinary opinion. If confirmed, Seller may offer a replacement kitten from a comparable future litter.

12. Buyer Responsibilities & Strict No-Declaw Policy

Buyer agrees to provide high-quality species-appropriate food, fresh water, a clean litter box, safe indoor housing, mental enrichment, and regular veterinary care. Buyer explicitly agrees NEVER to declaw the cat. Declawing constitutes mutilation and immediately voids all warranties and contracts.

13. No Rehoming or Surrender

Buyer may not sell, rehome, give away, or surrender the cat to any shelter or rescue organization. If Buyer cannot keep the cat at any point in its life, Seller must be contacted first for assistance in rehoming. Refunds are not guaranteed.

14. Breeder Right to Cancel

Seller may cancel the adoption before pickup if the home is deemed not a suitable fit or circumstances change affecting the kitten’s wellbeing. If Seller cancels, all Buyer payments are refunded in full.

15. What’s Included with Your Kitten

Age-appropriate FVRCP core vaccines, thorough deworming, written health record from licensed veterinarian, one free month of Trupanion pet insurance (buyer must activate within 24 hours of go-home), and lifetime breeder support. No microchip is provided routinely.

16. Weekly Photos & Progress Updates

Seller provides weekly photos or video updates when possible until pickup day so you can watch your kitten grow.

17. Entire Agreement

This contract represents the complete understanding between Buyer and Seller. Any changes or addendums must be in writing and signed by both parties.

Acknowledgment & Electronic Signature

A copy of your executed contract will be reviewed and archived for your kitten's veterinary record package.
