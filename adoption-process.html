Step-By-Step Journey

How Adoption Works

Six steps from your first application to a lifetime of breeder support.

01

Submit Your Application

Complete our adoption application form, telling us about your household, lifestyle, and what you’re looking for in a Maine Coon companion. We read every application personally.

02

Personal Conversation

We’ll reach out to schedule a call or video chat — your chance to ask every question and for us to learn about your family. No pressure, ever.

03

Matching & Deposit

Once we’ve found your ideal match from a current or upcoming litter, a deposit secures your kitten. You’ll receive regular photos and updates as they grow.

04

Pre-Departure Prep

We’ll share our recommended supplies list, nutrition guide, and everything you need to prepare. We want day one in your home to be smooth, joyful, and magical.

05

Homecoming at 12–14 Weeks

Your kitten arrives fully vaccinated, dewormed, microchipped, with complete health records and a written contract. This is a unforgettable day of celebration.

06

Lifetime of Support

Our relationship doesn’t end at adoption. We’re here for nutrition advice, health guidance, and celebrating every milestone — for the life of your cat.

✦ A $350 reservation fee secures your kitten

The reservation fee is fully applied toward your kitten’s total adoption purchase price upon pickup.
