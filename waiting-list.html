Adoption Roadmap

How Our Waiting List Works

Six simple steps from your application to bringing your kitten home.

01

Submit Your Application

Fill out the form below and let us know what kind of kitten you’re hoping for — including color, gender, personality, or special traits.

02

Reservation Fee

Once your application is approved, a $350 non-refundable reservation fee is required to secure your spot on the list. The reservation fee will be applied to the kitten price.

03

Priority Placement

You will be placed in line based on the date your reservation fee is received. Earlier reservations receive first choice when kittens become available.

04

Updates & Announcements

You’ll receive priority emails before kittens are publicly listed, including litter announcements, photos, and pairing updates.

05

Kitten Selection

When kittens are ready for reservation, we will contact you to match you with your preferred kitten or closest available match.

06

Final Adoption

Once your kitten is chosen, you will sign the adoption contract and pay the remaining balance before pickup.

Important note: If you choose to pass on a litter, you will remain on the list and move forward to the next available litter that fits your preferences.

✦ All of our breeding cats are registered with TICA (The International Cat Association).
