Planned Litters at Majestic Maine Coon Cats

Every pairing is chosen with care — for health, temperament, size, structure, and stunning color genetics, and in some litters, rare blue eyes or polydactyl paws. All of our parents are DNA-tested and TICA-registered, and every kitten is raised in our Canton, Georgia home. Kittens from these pairings often reserve quickly, so joining our waiting list gives you priority updates as details are confirmed.

How a planned litter works

Each pairing below is a plan, not a guarantee. Arrival dates are estimates, and the exact colors, eye colors and number of kittens are only known once the litter is born. When kittens arrive, we update this page and share first photos with families on our waiting list.

Reserving from a future litter

Start with our Kitten Application. Once you’re approved, a $350 reservation fee holds your place on the waiting list and goes toward your kitten’s price. Families are offered kittens in waiting list order, and every kitten goes home at 12–14 weeks with registration papers, two rounds of vaccines and our 1-year health guarantee.
