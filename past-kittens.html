These Faces Remind Us Why We Do What We Do

Our past Majestic Maine Coon kittens now live with families across Georgia and throughout the United States. Their beautiful colors, expressive features, and unique personalities reflect the quality we strive for in every litter.

Each one came from DNA-tested European parents and went home with registration papers, two rounds of vaccines and our 1-year health guarantee. Browse the gallery below to see the colors our lines produce, from black smoke and shaded silver to red, blue and tortie.
