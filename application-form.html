Before You Apply

What We Look For

We review every application personally. We’re looking for families who are committed, informed, and ready to provide a Maine Coon with the love and care they deserve.

“I don’t just place kittens — I place family members.” — Pili, Founder

✦ Key Adoption Criteria

  • ✦ A safe, loving indoor home environment with dedicated vertical play spaces
  • ✦ Commitment to regular veterinary care & annual health checkups
  • ✦ Understanding of Maine Coon needs: brushing, nutrition, companionship
  • ✦ Agreement to spay/neuter (for pet-only placements by 6 months)
  • ✦ Willingness to honor our adoption contract and never declaw
✓ We review every application and respond within 24–48 hours
