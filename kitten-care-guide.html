Raising Your Maine Coon

Maine Coon Kitten Care Guide

Your complete, veterinarian-informed roadmap for raising a healthy, happy, and magnificent European Maine Coon.

Preparing for Your Majestic Kitten

Welcome to our care guide — your complete, veterinarian-informed roadmap for raising a healthy, confident, and well-socialized Maine Coon kitten. With the right setup and daily care, your Maine Coon kitten will adjust quickly, feel safe and loved, and grow into a thriving, affectionate companion for many years to come.

Chapter 01

1. Pre-Arrival Setup

Pro Tip: Prepare a quiet safe room (spare bedroom or office). It speeds adjustment and protects your kitten while they learn the sounds and smells of your home.

✓Small space with door; hide-spots (covered bed or cardboard cave)
✓Food & water stations separated from litter by 6–8 ft
✓Low-sided litter pan (unscented clumping litter) + litter mat
✓Scratching options: vertical post (30"+ tall) and horizontal sisal pad
✓Warm bedding; soft blanket with familiar scent (we send one home with kitten)
✓Interactive toys put away after play; safe solo chew items only
✓Room balanced between stimulation and calm — no loud TVs or bustling foot traffic
Chapter 02

2. The First 72 Hours

Expect gradual exploring. Keep doors and windows closed and other pets separated. Go slow with early introductions to prevent sensory overload.

Day 1: Unbox gently in safe room. Show litter box, fresh water, and a small meal. Keep visits short, soft-spoken, and gentle.
Day 2: Short play sessions with a feather wand on the floor. Maintain the exact same food brand & litter we used.
Day 3: Begin brief, positive scent swaps and cracked-door introductions to other pets. Reward calm behavior with treats.

* We send weekly pictures while kittens grow at our cattery. For biosecurity, no in-home visits occur until after first vaccinations.

Chapter 03

3. Feeding & Nutrition

Maine Coons mature slowly (up to 3–4 years of continuous bone and muscle development). For the first year, prioritize complete, growth-labeled nutrition rich in animal protein and moisture.

Age Stage What & How Often Dietary Notes
8–12 Weeks 3–4 small meals/day; mix of wet + quality kitten kibble Keep the same brands for first 10–14 days; change gradually if switching.
3–6 Months 3 meals/day; fresh water at 2+ stations Introduce puzzle feeders; avoid rapid free-feeding if weight climbs too quickly.
6–12 Months 2–3 meals/day; kitten growth formula Transition to young-adult formula at ~12–15 months if body condition is ideal.
12+ Months Adult maintenance; 2 meals/day + daily wet food For large frames, a high-quality all-life-stages or large-breed diet is appropriate.
Strictly Avoid: Onions, garlic, grapes, raisins, chocolate, xylitol, cooked bones, and seasoned human table scraps.
Chapter 04

4. Litter Training & Hygiene

Start with the same unscented clumping litter used at our cattery; place the pan in a quiet, low-noise area that is always accessible.

✦Scoop daily; perform a full wash and fresh litter replacement weekly.
✦Use enzymatic cleaner for any adjustment accidents — never bleach or ammonia-based cleaners.
✦Maine Coons need extra-large pans (at least 1.5× their body length) as their massive bodies mature.
Chapter 05

5. Grooming, Nails, Ears & Teeth

✦Brushing: Brush 2–3× weekly using a slicker brush and wide-toothed metal comb. Increase frequency during seasonal sheds.
✦Nail Trimming: Trim front claw tips every 10–14 days. Pair with high-value freeze-dried treats for positive association.
✦Ear Care: Check ears weekly. Wipe gently with cotton rounds and vet-approved feline cleanser (never insert cotton swabs into ear canal).
✦Dental Hygiene: Introduce tooth-brushing early with poultry-flavored enzymatic toothpaste; aim for 2–3× weekly.
Chapter 06

6. Veterinary Care, Vaccines & Insurance

• Follow your veterinarian's schedule for core booster vaccines (FVRCP) and parasite prevention.

• Spay/Neuter: Must be completed by 6 months of age as required by your purchase agreement. Your vet will advise you on the exact optimal timing.

• Insurance Included: We provide one free month of pet insurance through Trupanion. Activation details are given at pickup.

• Health Guarantee: Your 1-year genetic health guarantee terms are fully detailed inside your contract.

Chapter 07

7. Socialization, Enrichment & Training

✦Daily interactive play (10–15 minutes) with a wand toy; finish with food to fulfill natural predatory hunting instincts.
✦Provide vertical territory with tall, heavy-duty cat trees (60"+) and wide window perches.
✦Leash training is optional; use a secure H-style harness and practice indoors before venturing outside.
✦Use clicker training, treats, and affection to teach name recognition, recall, and carrier comfort.
Chapter 08

8. Home Safety, Toxic Items & Travel

✦Secure window screens, blind cords, and heavy recliners; block gaps behind washers, dryers, and ovens.
✦Deadly Toxic Plants: Lilies (all varieties are fatal to cats), pothos, philodendron, and dieffenbachia.
✦Travel Safety: Always transport in a hard or airline-certified carrier with a familiar scent blanket. Never allow loose in cars.
Chapter 09

9. Complete Supplies Checklist

✓Quality kitten food (wet + dry), stainless steel bowls, stainless water fountain
✓Low-sided kitten pan → XXL pan as they grow; unscented clumping litter
✓Hard carrier, slicker brush & metal comb, cat nail clippers, enzymatic toothpaste
✓Heavy cat tree (60"+), tall sturdy scratching post, feather wands, puzzle feeder
✓Enzymatic stain & odor cleaner, pet-safe wipes, cozy microfiber blankets
Chapter 10 · Urgent Signs

10. When to Call the Vet

⚠Lethargy, refusal to eat for >24 hours, or repeated vomiting/diarrhea
⚠Labored or open-mouth breathing, pale/blue gums, distended or hard abdomen
⚠Straining in the litter box without producing urine (medical emergency in males)
⚠Sudden, drastic personality or mobility changes that cause concern
Breeder Recommendations

Our Tried-and-True Essentials

The specific gear, food brands, and products we personally use and trust in our Canton cattery.

XXL Litter Box

After testing various options, we always stick with standard King-Size XXL litter pans (such as So Phresh King-Size), as Maine Coons quickly outgrow standard pans.

Stainless Food & Water Station

Stainless steel bowls prevent chin acne. For hydration, a stainless steel circulating fountain encourages constant drinking and protects kidney health.

Heavy-Duty Cat Tower

Standard cat trees tip over under 20-lb cats. Look for reinforced models with 6-8 inch sisal poles from Kitty Mansions, Cat Tree King, or heavy Amazon towers.

Food Brands We Feed

We feed Fancy Feast grilled or pâté canned wet food and Purina ONE Healthy Kitten dry food. Chewy.com is our preferred delivery source.

Toys & Freeze-Dried Treats

Plastic springs, crinkle balls, and wand lures. For training rewards, use single-ingredient freeze-dried chicken breast or salmon (Vital Essentials).

Immune & Calming Support

We recommend NuVet feline immune supplements (order code #982208) and Feliway calming pheromones for smooth arrival.
