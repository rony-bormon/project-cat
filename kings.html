Our Breeding Males

The Kings

Majestic, powerful, and gentle — our Kings represent the finest European bloodlines, chosen for breed type, temperament, and genetic excellence.

Our Selection Standard

Only the finest males earn a place here

Every King is carefully chosen for his genetic legacy, wide muzzle, strong chin, powerful bone structure, and that wild European expression. Temperament is non-negotiable — our Kings must be as gentle and social as they are magnificent.

All of our breeding males are DNA-tested for HCM, SMA, and PKD, and carry titles or lineages from Europe’s most respected catteries.

✦ What We Select For

  • ✦ Broad, strong muzzle & square chin for the authentic wild European look
  • ✦ High cheekbones & wild expression with alert, expressive eyes
  • ✦ Large, tufted ears with long lynx tips set high and wide
  • ✦ Heavy bone & large rectangular body with muscular stature
  • ✦ Dense, flowing coat with full ruff and magnificent plumed tail
  • ✦ Calm, confident & affectionate nature — dog-like companion souls

Genetic Excellence

Health Testing — Our Standard

Every King undergoes comprehensive genetic testing before joining our program. Results are available upon request and provided in your kitten’s documentation package.

Some of our Kings were tested through a lab that doesn’t offer online reports. Printed copies of their full results are given to every family, and we’re happy to share them before you reserve.

♥

HCM Clear

Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy

Screened negative for hereditary cardiac gene mutations common to Maine Coons.

⚡

SMA Clear

Spinal Muscular Atrophy

DNA tested non-carrier for neuromuscular genetic motor neuron degradation.

✦

PKD Clear

Polycystic Kidney Disease

Confirmed negative for cystic progressive renal illness markers across all bloodlines.
