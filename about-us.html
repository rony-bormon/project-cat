European Bloodlines

Preserving the Authentic European Standard

"We proudly specialize in European bloodlines, selecting our Kings and Queens from champion lineages across Germany, Poland, Russia, and Switzerland."

These lines give our cats a wilder, more expressive appearance and that unmistakable regal presence with prominent lynx tips, square boxy muzzles, and majestic dense coats.

We also thoughtfully produce kittens with polydactyl paws and the enchanting blue-eyed trait — always with health and temperament as the foundation.

Our Philosophy

Our Approach

Three core commitments that define every day of life at Majestic Maine Coon Cats.

01

Health Above All

Every breeding cat is DNA-tested for HCM, SMA, and PKD, with routine screenings and veterinary-guided care. Before going home, each kitten is vaccinated, dewormed and examined, and comes with a complete health record and a written contract.

02

Raised in Our Home

Never a cage. Our kittens are born into family life, handled daily and introduced to children, pets and household sounds. Climbing structures, tunnels and sensory toys build confidence, balance and that classic Maine Coon curiosity.

03

Nutrition & Development

Kittens move onto high-quality, breed-appropriate food for strong bones, a healthy coat and good digestion. From weaning to litter training, each one gets hands-on support so they grow into thriving, confident young cats.

Meet the Breeder

About Pili

My love for Maine Coons began with their unmistakable elegance and remarkable temperament. As I learned more about the breed, I knew I wanted to contribute to its future through thoughtful, ethical breeding. Majestic Maine Coon Cats was built from a genuine passion for producing beautiful, confident, and well-adjusted kittens who reflect the very best of this extraordinary breed.

I take a selective, health-forward approach to every pairing. Each king and queen is evaluated for structure, temperament and overall wellness before joining the program, and I work closely with trusted veterinarians to support healthy mothers and thriving kittens from the very beginning.

"Every kitten is cuddled, nurtured, and loved from day one in our home. When they go to their new homes, they carry that trust and warmth with them."

Our Cattery

Designed for Safety & Comfort

Every queen, king and kitten lives in a safe, fully indoor, climate-controlled space that we keep meticulously clean.

Private Nurseries

Eight dedicated nurseries that our queens take turns using when it's time to have their kittens. Each one has heating for newborns and age-appropriate enrichment, and kittens stay there until they're ready for supervised play.

Outdoor Access for Queens

Each nursery connects to a private, 100% enclosed outdoor area. It's predator- and escape-proof, with fresh air and natural light, right next to their kittens.

Private Suites for Our Kings

Large indoor suites with secure, weather-protected outdoor space keep our kings relaxed and healthy, and prevent unplanned breeding.
