Common Questions

Custom Engraving FAQs

What kind of photo works best?

Choose the original, highest-quality digital photo available. Bright, sharp images with your pet's eyes and face clearly illuminated in natural light produce the richest, most detailed wood burns. Avoid screenshots, heavy social media filters, motion blur, and photos where the subject is far away.

Does the photo background matter?

A simple background is helpful, but not required! We digitally crop and prepare every subject by hand, isolating your pet so the focus remains entirely on their expression.

Will every engraving look exactly the same?

No two pieces of wood are identical. Natural differences in tree grain, warmth, and density are part of the authentic character of a handmade engraving and make every single piece a true original.

Can you create something other than a pet portrait?

Yes! Themed engravings, family keepsakes, wedding signs, memorial plaques, and custom gifts can all be designed upon request.
