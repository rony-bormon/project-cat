Special Medical Placement

Eira – Ready Now

Born January 17, 2026 · Spayed · Fully Recovered & Thriving

Ready Now · Canton, GA

Pale Black Silver Female

$1,500 $3,200 Special Adoption Placement

Eira is a beautiful, sweet pale black silver girl who is ready for her forever home now.

As a young kitten she was diagnosed with FIP and completed a full course of veterinary antiviral treatment. She has fully recovered and is healthy, playful and thriving. She is already spayed. Because of her medical history, she is offered at a special price of $1,500 (regularly $3,200). Her complete veterinary records are available, and we are happy to answer any questions.

This is the first case of FIP in our cattery since we began in 2016. FIP develops when a very common feline virus changes inside an individual cat. It is not one of the inherited conditions we DNA-test for, such as HCM, SMA and PKD. Today there is an effective antiviral treatment for FIP, and many cats, like Eira, go on to make a full recovery.
