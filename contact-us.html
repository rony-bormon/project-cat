Good to Know Before You Write:

To protect the biosecurity and health of our kittens and moms, we do not offer in-home visits. We share photos, video clips, and live FaceTime updates as your kitten grows. Local pickup in Canton is preferred; we also meet within driving distance or coordinate cabin flight nannies. We never ship kittens as cargo.
