Our Breeding Females

The Queens

Rare bloodlines from across Europe. Each queen selected for exceptional type, health, and temperament.

Our Selection Standard

Only the finest females earn a place here

Our queens are sourced exclusively from champion bloodlines across Germany, Poland, Russia, and Switzerland. Every female is DNA-tested, health-screened, and carries documented European pedigrees.

Some of our Queens were tested through a lab that doesn’t offer online reports. Printed copies of their full results are given to every family, and we’re happy to share them before you reserve.

✦ What We Select For

  • ✦ DNA-tested for HCM, SMA & PKD before breeding
  • ✦ European champion pedigrees — TICA & CFA registered
  • ✦ Exceptional temperament — gentle, social, people-bonded mothers
  • ✦ Selected for rare traits — polydactyl paws & brilliant blue eyes

Preserving the True European Standard

Dedicated to Breed Preservation & Future Generations

Each queen represents the future of our bloodlines. With every litter, she carries forward the health, elegance, intelligence, and gentle spirit that Maine Coons are known for. Because we believe in preserving the true Maine Coon, we are committed to careful, ethical breeding practices that protect the breed’s legacy — not just today, but for years to come.

We are honored to share our Maine Coon Queens in Georgia with loving families across the Southeast and the United States.
